Questions

Before you apply.

Who do you actually work with?

A deliberately small roster. We take on creators where we can see a clear path to a real career. Not everyone who applies, and never more than the team can genuinely carry.

What does it cost?

We work on a revenue share, so we only earn when you do. Exact terms come out of the audit, once we both know what the work looks like.

Am I locked into a long contract?

No. Our retention number comes from people staying because it works, not because they can't leave.

Which platforms do you cover?

TikTok, Instagram and X are the core, with YouTube and newsletter formats where they fit the creator. We build platform-native, not copy-paste.

How fast do things start moving?

The audit is done within a week of applying, and the 90-day blueprint starts right after. First measurable movement usually lands inside the first month.
